Swag Scope

Online movie posters and music covers

Folder exploration automatically looks up film information and posters through Wikipedia and Wikidata, and album information and artwork through MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive. No account or API key is needed. The folder options menu can turn either lookup off.

Explore folders and archives

Opening a folder, or choosing Explore folder (Ctrl+E), displays its contents in the central area. ZIP files use the same navigation. Choose names, sortable details, or small, medium and large thumbnails. Double-click or press Enter to open an item; closing its viewer returns to the explorer with the selection and display mode preserved.

Enumeration, metadata, sorting, archive extraction and thumbnail decoding run asynchronously. A viewer can optionally provide Viewer.Plugin.thumbnail as a direct image decoder; otherwise its ordinary viewer renders offscreen with Viewer.Host.isThumbnail() set.
