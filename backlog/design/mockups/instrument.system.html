Six laws

01

One lit plane

The document is the only light surface. Bar, panels and readout line stay on the ink: the eye goes straight to the content.

02

One bar, one line

The caption carries the document, the search and the viewer. Everything measured fits in 28 units at the bottom.

03

The notch

The only permanent Voltage: 64 units cut at 45° on the top edge, above the mark. It replaces the full-width rail.

04

Words in sans, numbers in mono

Inter for what is read, Roboto Mono for what is measured: offsets, sizes, positions, counts, durations.

05

Arc chooses, Voltage acts

Selection, hover, links: Arc. Primary action, current hit, cursor: Voltage. Never both on one object.

06

Content touches the edges

No card set on a ground, no decorative margin. Tone separates first, air second, a hairline last.
