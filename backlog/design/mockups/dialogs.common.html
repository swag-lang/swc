The rules of the box

The body is the lit plane. Caption and action bar stay on the ink, exactly like a window's bar and readout line.

One Voltage per box. The default action is filled, the others carry a hairline. Keyboard focus is a Voltage ring.

Severity is a glyph. 20 units, in its tone's mark (info, caution, critical), left of the first sentence. The technical detail is a second line in mono.

A panel with its own ground reaches the frame. The file browser takes no inset: places on the ink, list on the plane.

Fields are hollowed out. Ink ground on the plane, a one-step edge; a form aligns its labels on one column.

The measures stay the theme's. Only the layers' colors change: no box writes a number.
