# Third-party notices

The native Swag AAC-LC decoder and its normative lookup tables were cross-checked against the
clean-room [glint](https://github.com/CrispStrobe/glint) implementation at commit
`77738f3ed9b15f627196cc5bbd7f6406814ba2fb`. glint is not compiled, linked, or shipped as a
runtime dependency.

The native Swag AC-3 and E-AC-3 decoders were cross-checked against
[ac3.js](https://github.com/DanielleHuisman/ac3.js) at commit
`b6de7f6ab35bf3b9f9c24653ab4e0bcf746d5133` and
[oxideav-ac3](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-ac3) at commit
`8acf106d50d58f359946c086d1b393a060eaf5f6`. Neither project is compiled, linked, or shipped as
a runtime dependency; the production implementation and tables are Swag source in this module.
