    func Face.hintVertical[src]

    Vertical grid-fitting (blue zones): snap the baseline, x-height and cap-height of the currently-loaded outline to whole pixels so horizontal stems stay crisp. No-op when the face has no x-height (OS/2 missing). Apply before rendering the bitmap; it must not be used on the size-independent SDF/MSDF atlases.

    mtd hintVertical() fail
