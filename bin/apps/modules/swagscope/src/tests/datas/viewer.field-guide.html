Sampling routine

Visit the marked boardwalk stations in order. Record the time, air temperature, water level and any visible change before taking a sample. Use the same marked depth at every visit so seasonal readings can be compared.

When equipment needs repair, note the station, date and reading conditions in the maintenance log; do not fill a missing measurement with an estimate.

Safety and access

Stay on the raised path when the marsh is flooded. Work in pairs near the eastern gate, carry a charged phone and report a washed-out boardwalk before crossing the affected area.

Stop work if lightning is heard or water reaches the marked closure line. Resume only after the route lead confirms access is safe.

After heavy rain

Check the lower crossing from the signed lookout. Photograph visible changes from the public path and add a note for the next visit.
